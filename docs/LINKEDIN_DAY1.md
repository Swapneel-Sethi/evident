# LinkedIn — Day 1 (build-in-public)

We gave an AI agent a job description and three resumes.

Instead of asking "who is the best candidate?", we asked "what can you actually prove?"

That question became EVIDENT — evidence-grounded recruitment intelligence, our build for the Product Space Agentic AI Hackathon.

The insight: recruiting doesn't have a resume-reading problem. It has an evidence problem. JD, resumes and interview notes live disconnected, and recruiters manually rebuild the links every time.

Day 1 shipped: workspace + JD/resume upload, JD → structured requirements, resume → evidence mapped per requirement, missing info flagged as validation gaps, interview questions generated per gap.

No scores. No rankings. Just: requirement → claim → source → status → question.

Tomorrow: the interview loop — answers update the evidence live. Follow the build. #BuildInPublic #AgenticAI #Hackathon
